The HokMob Rating

How it works

Every dressed player in a live or past game is rated from 0 to 10 on the spot by HokMob's algorithm. Everyone starts at 5, an average game, and then gets weighted terms added for each stat in the box score. You can build a stat line and watch the rating come together term by term as you go.
