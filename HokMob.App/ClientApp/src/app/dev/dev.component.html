The HokMob rating

Every dressed player in a live or past game is rated from 0 to 10 by StatsUtils.calculateSkaterHokmobRating and calculateGoalieHokMobRating. Both start at 5, an average game, and add a weighted term for each stat in the boxscore. Build a stat line on the left and watch the rating come together on the right.
